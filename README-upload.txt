TaskPad website — Safa Devs

Upload index.html, privacy.html, taskpad-logo.png and favicon.png into the ROOT of your public GitHub repository named taskpad under safatech-af.

Then go to Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root) > Save.

After deployment:
Homepage: https://safatech-af.github.io/taskpad/
Privacy: https://safatech-af.github.io/taskpad/privacy.html

Before submitting to Google Play, check the privacy policy against the release app and update it whenever data practices change.
