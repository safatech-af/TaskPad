Privacy Policy

TaskPad · Safa Devs · Updated November 11, 2026

Safa Devs ("we", "us") develops TaskPad (the "App"), a task management application for Android and iOS. This policy describes how information is handled when you use the App. TaskPad is designed to store your tasks on your device. Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup are optional.

1. Information you choose to add

TaskPad processes the information you choose to enter or attach, including task names, descriptions, dates, statuses, checklists, manually entered or selected locations, photos, and information used to generate reports. Task records and attached photos are stored locally on your device. This information is used to provide the features you request.

2. Google Sign-In and optional Google Drive backup

Google Sign-In is optional; you can use TaskPad without signing in. If you choose Google Drive backup or restore, TaskPad requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file to create and access TaskPad files in your Google Drive, including backups, photos, and report PDFs. This scope provides access to files created or opened through the app, not unrestricted access to the rest of your Drive. Files are stored in regular Google Drive files or folders. On Android, sign-in also requests openid, email, and profile to display basic account information such as your name and email address. On iOS, the Drive authorization request uses drive.file. Google handles account authentication and Drive storage according to its privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Photos and location details

Task photos are stored locally on your device and included in optional Google Drive backups. You may enter or select task locations. When you type an address, TaskPad sends the address text to Google’s geocoding service on Android or Apple’s mapping service on iOS to provide address suggestions. These providers process the query under their own privacy practices. TaskPad does not continuously track your device location as part of this feature.

4. Reminders and notifications

TaskPad uses the task dates and reminder settings you choose to provide notifications. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings. The app does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services for notifications in the current release.

5. Analytics, crash reports and other services

TaskPad does not currently integrate analytics, third-party crash-reporting, advertising, or attribution SDKs. The app may use third-party services to deliver features you choose, including Google Sign-In, Google Drive, and address suggestions from Google or Apple. We may introduce analytics in a future release; before doing so, we will update this policy and relevant app-store disclosures and obtain any consent required by law.

6. Sharing and disclosure

Task information is stored on your device by default. If you enable Google Drive backup, TaskPad uploads backed-up task information, photos, and report PDFs to your Google Drive. Drafts are not uploaded as part of the behavior reviewed for this policy. When you type an address, the text is sent to Google on Android or Apple on iOS to provide suggestions. If you use your device’s share sheet, the task or PDF you choose to share is passed to the destination you select. We do not sell your task information or display advertising.

Third-party privacy information: Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

7. Retention and deletion

Task information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app’s local data. Moving a task to Trash does not immediately remove its Google Drive copy. TaskPad attempts to remove the associated Drive data after the task is permanently deleted, Trash is emptied, or the task is purged after 30 days, on the next successful synchronization.

Cloud deletion requires network connectivity, valid authorization, and a successful Google Drive operation. If deletion fails, the Drive copy may remain; the current app may not notify you of every failed deletion attempt. If you need to ensure a copy is gone, check the TaskPad files in your Google Drive and remove remaining files there, or contact us for help. Uninstalling TaskPad or clearing local data alone does not necessarily remove Google Drive files.

8. Security

We use the app’s local storage and Google’s authorization and Drive services to provide the features described above. We aim to use appropriate safeguards to protect your information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

TaskPad is a general-purpose task-management app for users of different ages and is not specifically directed at children. Parents or guardians may contact us about privacy questions at the email below. The age groups selected in app-store declarations must accurately reflect the app’s intended audience.

10. Your privacy choices and rights

You can use TaskPad without Google Sign-In, choose whether to use Google Drive backup, and manage app permissions through your device settings. You can delete information through the app as described above. Depending on where you live, privacy laws may provide rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. Contact us if you need help with a privacy request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when TaskPad's features or data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
